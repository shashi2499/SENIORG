- The **Assisted Desk** and **Family Circle** are shown *inside* the hospital journey, where a real reason exists for them, instead of as separate tours.
- **Smart Minutes** is introduced through a suspicious call, which shows why verified learning belongs in the product.
- **House help** is added, because it is a hero journey and shows a multi-day service.
